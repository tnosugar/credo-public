Landing-Page Index

Verbatim scrapes of the 36 Credo Google Ads landing pages currently live at start.credolegal.com, grouped by vertical. This is a staging catalog — not a real website. Pages link out to the live URL, to their scraped HTML, and to the source copy doc in _intake/.

Every LP conforms to the 20-section / 8-CTA template in specs/landing-page-template.md. Verticals below map to Google Ads campaign structure, not to hero-pattern angles (which cut across verticals).

Content notes from the scrape

  • Slug / content mismatches. Two live URLs serve content unrelated to their slug — /credit-card-debt-challenge and /debt-harassment-act-fast both render lien-removal copy ("We Can Help Remove Judgment or Creditor Liens"). Likely a Webflow repurposing; audit before rewriting either.
  • Source-doc URL typo. LP_MedDebt-Lawsuit_VarA_Urgency lists its URL as /medica-debt-lawsuit-respond (404); the live page is at /medical-debt-lawsuit-respond. Index uses the working URL.
  • Shared URL. /wage-garnishment-attorney is referenced by two source docs (LP_AllStates-Garn_VarA_Fight and LP_PostJudgment-Garn_VarA_FightActive). Both listed below; only one file scraped.
  • Stale artefact. website/lp/medica-debt-lawsuit-respond.html contains the 404 page from the initial scrape attempt. Needs manual deletion (sandbox can't remove it); not linked from the index.
